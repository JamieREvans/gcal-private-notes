# Private Event Notes for Google Calendar: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

Private Event Notes is a browser extension, made by an independent hobby developer, that lets you keep private notes on your Google Calendar events. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

## What the extension stores

- **Your notes**, plus the title, time and link of the event each note belongs to. These are stored in your browser's extension storage on your device.
- **Your settings and templates**, stored the same way.

## Where your notes go

- **Nowhere, by default.** Notes are never sent to the developer or to any server, and they are never written into your Google Calendar events. Event guests and organisers cannot see them.
- **Browser sync (optional, Pro).** If you turn on "Sync notes across browsers", your most recent notes are also saved in your browser's built-in sync storage (for Chrome, this is your Google account's Chrome Sync). The developer cannot access that data.
- **Exports.** If you export your notes, a file is saved to your computer. What happens to it after that is up to you.

## What the extension reads

On `calendar.google.com`, the extension reads the event you open (its identifier, title and time) so it can attach your note to the right event and show it in the right place. It reads nothing else and sends nothing anywhere.

## Payments

Pro upgrades are processed by [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com) and [Stripe](https://stripe.com). When you pay, they handle your email address and payment details under their own privacy policies. The extension asks ExtensionPay only whether your copy has been paid for. Your notes are never shared with them.

## Analytics and tracking

None. The extension contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

## Deleting your data

Use **Settings → Delete all notes**, or uninstall the extension, which removes its local storage. Turning sync off removes your notes from browser sync. Copies already on your other devices stay there until you delete them on those devices.
